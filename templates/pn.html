About the Project

Forecasting Demand for Ola Bike Rides

This project leverages advanced machine learning techniques to predict the number of ride requests in specific time periods and locations. Our system analyzes historical data including:

  • Time-based patterns and seasonality
  • Weather conditions and temperature
  • Holiday and working day patterns
  • Atmospheric conditions and humidity
  • Wind speed and environmental factors

The goal is to enhance operational efficiency for Ola by optimizing resource allocation, improving customer experience, and minimizing costs through proactive planning based on predicted ride demand.
